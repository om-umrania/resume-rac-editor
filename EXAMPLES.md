# RAC in practice

All examples below are fictional. Numbers illustrate the writing pattern and must never be copied as personal achievements.

## 1. When you have a measured outcome

**Before:** Prepared reports and worked on automation

**After:** **Saved 12 reporting hours monthly** by automating weekly Excel reports for a regional sales team

Evidence to establish: how the time saving was measured, which tasks it covers, the monthly period and your contribution to the automation

## 2. When the result is delivery

**Before:** Worked on a chatbot project

**After:** **Delivered a support-assistant prototype** by building a searchable FAQ workflow for an internal help desk

This describes what was delivered. It does not claim adoption, ticket reduction or production use without evidence.

## 3. When you worked with a team

**Before:** Independently built a recommendation platform

**After:** **Delivered a recommendation proof of concept** by **leading four interns** on requirements, architecture and matching logic

Use this wording only if you led that team. If you contributed as an individual team member, describe the specific component or work you owned.

## 4. When the evidence is incomplete

**Draft claim:** Increased conversion by 30%

Before using it, establish:

- Was this a 30% relative lift or a 30 percentage-point increase?
- What were the baseline and final values?
- Over what period and population was it measured?
- What did you personally change?
- Does the evidence support attribution to that change?

Until resolved, keep it in a separate review note. Use a supported delivery or action bullet if one is available.

## Small edits that matter

| Avoid | Prefer | Why |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Saved 12 hours | Saved 12 reporting hours monthly | Names the activity and time unit |
| **12** hours saved | **Saved 12 reporting hours monthly** | Makes the emphasis meaningful on its own |
| Launched an enterprise platform | Delivered an internal prototype | Keeps scope accurate when only a prototype exists |
| Worked on various improvements | A specific contribution and supported result | Makes the work assessable |
| Shrinking the font to fit | Tightening wording or splitting distinct ideas | Preserves readability |

Rendered width depends on the template, font and bolding. These examples demonstrate wording, not guaranteed single-line fit in your document.
